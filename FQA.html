Common Questions

Q1. How do I log in?

Use your Student ID and Password to log in.


Q2. How can I check my attendance?

Open the Attendance page from the Dashboard.


Q3. Where can I view my assignments?

Go to the Assignments page after logging in.


Q4. How do I check my results?

Click on the Results option from the Dashboard.


Q5. How can I submit feedback?

Open the Feedback page, fill out the form, and click Submit.
